# Lead Conversion Center Implementation SOP

Internal operator procedure for deploying Lead Conversion Center for a contractor org.

## Steps

1. Confirm the correct org/tenant is selected.
2. Confirm plan/module access if gating exists.
3. Choose the trade template.
4. Configure business settings and service area.
5. Configure the lead capture form.
6. Configure lead source adapters or source labels.
7. Review scoring keywords and high-value services.
8. Review SMS and email templates.
9. Review follow-up sequence timing and channels.
10. Keep dry-run enabled.
11. Seed demo leads or create a staff-owned test lead.
12. Verify the Lead Conversion Center dashboard.
13. Verify hot lead scoring.
14. Verify follow-up tasks.
15. Verify conversion to customer/job.
16. Confirm provider readiness.
17. Send test SMS/email only through the explicit provider test flow.
18. Run the production readiness checklist.
19. Schedule go-live with a rollback owner.
20. Monitor the first week.
21. Document handoff.

## Operator notes

- Do not turn off dry-run until the Go Live Checklist passes.
- Do not send live lead messages from normal lead actions during setup.
- Use staff-owned contact information for tests.
- Do not paste provider secrets into notes, browser fields, or screenshots.
- If a checklist item is unclear, leave dry-run on and escalate before launch.

## Evidence to capture

- Screenshot of module/readiness status.
- Screenshot of lead capture form status.
- Screenshot of Go Live Checklist.
- Test lead name and created timestamp.
- Dry-run message activity.
- Test SMS/email result.
- Client handoff date.
- First-week review date.
