# Lead Conversion Center Client Handoff

This guide is for contractor staff using TradeFlowKit day to day.

## What it does

Lead Conversion Center helps your team capture leads, see who needs attention first, follow up before leads go cold, and turn qualified leads into customers and jobs.

## How to open it

1. Log in to TradeFlowKit.
2. Open **Leads** from the navigation.
3. Use the top dashboard to review hot leads, follow-ups, recent leads, and recent conversions.

## Dashboard sections

- **Hot leads:** Leads that look urgent, high value, or ready for fast contact.
- **Needs contact:** New leads that have not been contacted yet.
- **Follow-ups due today:** Leads that need a follow-up today.
- **Overdue:** Follow-ups that are past due.
- **Recently converted:** Leads that became customers and jobs.
- **Needs attention:** Failed or blocked message/follow-up attempts.

## What hot leads mean

Hot leads are prioritized based on urgency, service type, description, and expected value. Review these first when the office opens or when a new lead arrives.

## What follow-up due means

Follow-up due means TradeFlowKit expects someone to contact the lead. The goal is to keep every opportunity visible until it is won, lost, or no longer valid.

## Create a manual lead

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Click **New Lead**.
3. Enter name and at least one contact method.
4. Add service type, urgency, notes, and SMS consent if available.
5. Save the lead.

## Review lead detail

Open a lead card or row to see:

- Contact information.
- Source.
- Service request.
- Score and urgency.
- Follow-up tasks.
- Message history.
- Activity timeline.
- Conversion actions.

## Send or prepare a message

If the account is in dry-run mode, message buttons create prepared activity only. If the account is live, messages may send only when the provider, template, recipient, and consent checks pass.

## Dry-run vs live

- **Dry-run:** Messages are logged but not sent.
- **Live:** Messages may be sent to real leads, subject to safety checks.

Leave dry-run on unless the business owner or implementation team has completed the go-live checklist.

## Convert a lead to customer/job

1. Open the lead.
2. Confirm the request is qualified.
3. Click **Convert**.
4. TradeFlowKit creates or links the customer and creates a job lead.
5. Continue the normal quote/job/invoice process.

## Mark a lead lost or spam

- Mark a lead **lost** when the customer chose another provider or the job is not moving forward.
- Mark a lead **spam** when it is not a real opportunity.

## Check linked customer/job

After conversion, review the customer and job records from the normal Customers and Jobs sections.

## If a lead looks wrong

- Check the source.
- Correct the service type or urgency.
- Re-score the lead.
- Add an internal note in the activity timeline.
- Contact support if the same issue repeats.

## If follow-ups are overdue

- Open the overdue lead.
- Contact the lead or mark it lost/spam.
- Update the next follow-up date if the opportunity is still active.
- Review staffing if overdue follow-ups are recurring.

## Support

Contact your TradeFlowKit support contact for setup changes, source issues, messaging changes, or go-live questions.

## Small appendix

Some leads may come from connected source endpoints. Staff do not need to manage the technical connection day to day; just review whether the lead source label looks correct.
