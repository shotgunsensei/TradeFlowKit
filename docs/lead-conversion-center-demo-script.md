# Lead Conversion Center Demo Script

## Contractor Pain-Point Framing

Most contractors do not lose jobs because they cannot do the work. They lose jobs because the lead got missed, the office called back too late, the follow-up lived in somebody's head, or the quote/job handoff was too manual. Lead Conversion Center is the front door for that workflow.

Use plain language:

- Book more jobs.
- Know who to call first.
- Follow up before leads go cold.
- Capture every website, phone, and manual lead in one place.
- Convert qualified leads into jobs without retyping customer details.

Avoid over-explaining:

- Adapter payloads.
- Webhook processing.
- Messaging providers.
- Database tables.
- Automation internals.

## What To Show First

Open `/leads/demo` or click **Demo Walkthrough** on `/leads`.

- Needs Contact
- Follow Up Today

## 3-Minute Demo Script

1. **Problem**
   - "Contractors miss revenue when a form, call, or message does not become a booked job quickly."

2. **Capture**
   - Show the lead capture form/link.
   - "Website leads, missed-call leads, manual leads, and source links all land here."

3. **Prioritize**
   - Show a hot lead.
   - "Urgent work rises to the top so the office knows who to call first."

4. **Follow up**
   - Show follow-ups due today or overdue follow-ups.
   - "Follow-ups are visible before the lead goes cold."

5. **Safe messaging**
   - Show dry-run mode.
   - "Messages are prepared and logged. They do not send until live messaging is explicitly enabled and provider checks pass."

6. **Convert**
   - Open a qualified lead and show Convert.
   - "When the customer is ready, this creates or reuses the customer and opens a job lead."

7. **Setup**
   - Show the setup checklist.
   - "The launch path is clear: choose trade, configure form, review templates, confirm dry-run/live mode, then create or receive the first lead."

## 10-Minute Demo Script

1. **Set context**
   - Ask what lead sources they use today: website form, missed calls, referrals, Facebook messages, Google forms, dispatch calls, or manual notes.

2. **Show module status**
   - Explain current mode: Not Set Up, Demo Mode, Dry-Run, Live, or Needs Attention.
   - Show the next best action.

3. **Run the guided walkthrough**
   - Step through all six guided demo steps.
   - Keep the focus on office workflow, not implementation.

4. **Open the capture form**
   - Show the public form URL and embed snippet.
   - Explain that public responses return only a safe success message.

5. **Open a hot lead**
   - Point out source, urgency, service type, score, age, next follow-up, and contact indicators.

6. **Show follow-up safety**
   - Open Lead Settings.
   - Show dry-run mode, provider readiness, SMS opt-out wording, and test message controls.

7. **Convert to job**
   - Show the conversion section.
   - Explain that the existing customer/job/quote/invoice workflow stays intact.

8. **Review setup checklist**
   - Show which steps are incomplete.
   - Explain what your onboarding service will configure.

9. **Close with outcome**
   - "This gives your office one place to see who needs contact, who needs follow-up, and which leads are turning into booked work."

## Objection Handling

**"We already have a contact form."**

Your form collects information. This turns the request into a lead pipeline with scoring, follow-up, activity history, and customer/job conversion.

**"I do not want automatic texts going out."**

Dry-run is the default. Messages are prepared and logged but not sent unless live messaging is explicitly enabled and safety checks pass.

**"We get most leads by phone."**

Missed-call and manual lead capture still feed the same pipeline. The goal is one workflow for every lead source.

**"My office staff is not technical."**

The operator view uses contractor-friendly labels: Hot Leads, Needs Contact, Follow Up Today, Recently Converted, and Needs Attention.

**"Can this connect to Zapier, Make, or n8n?"**

Generic source endpoints are available for posting leads into TradeFlowKit. Full external platform ownership stays outside the system; TradeFlowKit remains the source of truth.

## Vertical Pitch Angles

### HVAC

Lead with emergency no-cooling/no-heat response. Emphasize hot lead scoring, same-day callback, replacement signals, and seasonal rush follow-up.

### Electrical

Lead with safety and high-value work: no power, panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, and commercial wiring.

### Plumbing

Lead with urgent leaks, clogs, water heaters, and callbacks before the homeowner calls the next plumber.

### Roofing

Lead with storm damage, leaks, inspections, photos, and follow-up after estimate requests.

### Landscaping

Lead with recurring maintenance, seasonal cleanup, irrigation, and property-size qualification.

### General Contractor

Lead with remodel scope qualification, budget/timeline capture, and converting serious inquiries into jobs.

### IT Field Service

Lead with outage urgency, onsite dispatch, device count, affected users, and fast qualification before scheduling.

## Do Not Claim

- Guaranteed revenue increases.
- Guaranteed response-time outcomes.
- Fake customer results.
- That messages send automatically by default.
- That external ad platforms are fully integrated before provider-specific work is built.
